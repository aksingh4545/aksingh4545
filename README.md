## 👋 A bit about me

I work at the intersection of **data engineering**, **cloud platforms**, and **real-time systems**.

Most days you'll find me designing reliable data pipelines, taming messy datasets, optimizing Spark jobs, and thinking about how systems behave at scale.

I believe in building systems that are:
- **Predictable**
- **Observable**
- **Easy to maintain & reason about**

Clean logs, calm infrastructure, and well-documented workflows make me happy.
